About ScoreHub

ScoreHub is a free live-scores site. It shows today's matches, results and upcoming fixtures for football, basketball, hockey, tennis and cricket, updated every few seconds while games are in play.

What you can do here

  • Follow live scores and match status for the leagues and tournaments we cover, and browse yesterday's and tomorrow's games.
  • Open a football match for the summary timeline with goals, cards and substitutions, team statistics, line-ups with player ratings, and head-to-head history.
  • Read league tables for the major football competitions.
  • Watch the official highlights of finished matches, where one is available, from the league, broadcaster or club's own YouTube channel.
  • Follow your football teams and turn on alerts. You get a notification on your phone or browser for their goals, kick-off, full time and a reminder before they play.

Where the information comes from

Scores, fixtures and tables come from third-party sports data providers, including football-data.org, API-Football and public sports feeds. Highlight videos are embedded from YouTube and belong to their uploaders. Team names and crests belong to their owners and are shown for identification only.

Accuracy

We work to keep scores current, but data providers can be delayed or wrong, so a score shown here should not be treated as official. If you notice a mistake, please tell us.

Cost

ScoreHub is free to use. It may show advertising to help cover the cost of running it.
